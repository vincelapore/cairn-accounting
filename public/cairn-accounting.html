About Cairn

Built for small
business. Full stop.

Cairn was started because most accounting firms are built around big corporate clients — small businesses get the junior staff and the templated advice. We flipped that.

Every client at Cairn works directly with a qualified accountant who knows their business personally. We don't farm your work out. We don't disappear after tax time. We're here when you have questions, year-round.

Based in Brisbane's inner suburbs, we work with trades, hospitality, healthcare, retail, and professional services across South East Queensland.
